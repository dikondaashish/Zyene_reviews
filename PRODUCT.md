# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Local business owners, multi-location operators, managers, and agencies managing reputation workflows. They work inside one organization and one location at a time to monitor reviews, respond, request new reviews, manage customers, and track local visibility.

## Product Purpose

Zyene Reviews gives location-based businesses one reliable workspace for review aggregation, responses, SMS and email campaigns, private feedback, competitor tracking, local SEO signals, team access, and billing. Success means users can understand the active tenant and location immediately, trust the data, and complete routine reputation work without ambiguity.

## Brand Personality

Warm, approachable, and professional. The interface should feel organized and human while remaining efficient for repeated operational use.

## Anti-references

Avoid cold monochrome developer-tool styling, decorative glass effects, excessive shadows, generic SaaS card grids, ambiguous labels, and visual novelty that slows task completion. Do not use decoration where a clear label or familiar control is sufficient.

## Design Principles

1. Make organization and location context unmistakable before users act.
2. Prefer clear operational language over internal system terminology.
3. Keep repeated workflows compact, predictable, and easy to scan.
4. Use warm, restrained visual structure with borders and focused accent color.
5. Surface trustworthy state and actionable recovery when data or integrations fail.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, visible focus states, keyboard-accessible interactions, sufficient contrast, and touch-friendly targets. Respect reduced-motion preferences and never rely on color alone to communicate state.
