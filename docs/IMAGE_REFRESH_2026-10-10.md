# Image refresh — October 10, 2026

Reviewed all 106 repository-owned image files present at baseline commit `eae71ae599bee025bdb629af95153d66b493fa9a`: 105 raster images and one SVG. Generated and integrated 47 replacement scenes with the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool. This covers 36 blog images, six industry scenes, three shared marketing scenes, and two portraits used only in labelled examples. The 57 older file paths now serve compatibility versions of the same replacements. The other 49 original files were retained.

The decision was based on visual appearance: overly smooth people, artificial lighting, implausible scenes, and repetitive stock-style staging. Appearance alone does not prove how an original image was produced. The new pictures are still AI-generated illustrations, with fictional people. They are not customer proof, real staff portraits, provider screenshots, or evidence of business results.

## Scope and retained imagery

- The asset inventory, original sizes, dispositions, new hashes, consumer files, and all 47 final prompts are in [IMAGE_REFRESH_MANIFEST_2026-10-10.json](IMAGE_REFRESH_MANIFEST_2026-10-10.json).
- The four existing documentary photographs and their recorded credits remain unchanged: café service, café conversation, restaurant guests, and window installation. See [marketing-photography.md](marketing-photography.md).
- Logos, icons, the approved Zyene mascot, NFC product photos, the designed guide cover, decorative plant/mug cutouts, and the SVG widget preview keep their existing roles.
- Unused earlier portraits and restaurant/home-services source pictures were inspected but left in place. Their old registry entries are not used by current rendered pages. Current testimonial authors use initials. This task does not remove unused files.
- Runtime social-preview images are designed typography/brand graphics generated by Next.js, rather than synthetic business photographs. No design change was needed.
- Customer-uploaded photos, business logos, and remote reviewer avatars are supplied by customer/platform data. This review did not inspect tenant records or replace that content. Remote brand logos are also outside the generated-photo refresh.

## Integration decisions

New `-candid-v2.webp` URLs make the refresh visible through image caches. Existing WebP, JPG, and PNG URLs are preserved as compatibility copies with their original dimensions. The existing legacy blog JPG redirects remain valid. All source references, blog cover/body mappings, shared marketing consumers, and industry catalog image paths use the new pictures.

Photo descriptions now describe visible scenes without claiming unreadable screens show Google interfaces or the Zyene dashboard. The dashboard-themed article image is explicitly labelled illustrative. Captions attached to replaced images no longer use unsupported numerical response-rate claims as photo descriptions; this is not a complete factual audit of the surrounding articles. Existing example feedback and composite-workflow labels remain intact.

The shared industry mapping previously fell back to missing `/images/industries/hvac.webp`, `plumbing.webp`, and `electricians.webp`. These three pages now use the existing home-service photograph, matching their catalog image paths. This is general trade imagery; the alt text accurately describes a window installation.

The homepage closing-photo crop was moved toward the new subject. Shared team and industry-photo crops were adjusted after browser review to keep faces visible in wide cards. Other compositions keep their subjects near the center for square and wide responsive cards. Six blog scenes were regenerated after the visual review found repetitive faces and settings.
