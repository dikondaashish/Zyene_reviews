# Zyene Reviews: 90-day backlink plan

Prepared September 13, 2026. This is an execution plan; no outreach, account creation, paid placement, or directory submission has been performed.

## Starting point

Search Console reports **one external link**, from **zyene.com**, pointing to the homepage. It reports 234 internal links. These are Google's reported links, not an exhaustive backlink inventory. Build relevant third-party references and useful referral traffic rather than increasing raw link counts.

No backlink-database export was available for this audit. Domain authority, followed-link share, competitor intersections, link velocity, and toxicity scores are unmeasured; no disavow action is warranted by the evidence gathered.

## Days 1–14: establish accurate product profiles

| Action | Destination | Evidence to prepare | Success measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Search for an existing profile, then claim or request one | [G2 product profiles](https://sell.g2.com/create-a-profile) | Actual product screenshots, accurate feature list, public pricing, canonical website URL | One accurate profile, no duplicates |
| Check eligibility and submit an accurate listing | [Capterra vendor portal](https://www.capterra.com/vendors/) | Same product facts; follow [listing guidelines](https://www.capterra.com/legal/listing-guidelines/) | Listing accepted; track referrals if a website link is provided |
| Review the existing parent-company reference | zyene.com | Confirm the existing link points directly to https://www.zyenereviews.com/ and explains the product | Useful, working branded reference |
| Prepare a reusable press/product facts sheet | Internal sales/growth document | Founder-approved company facts, logo, screenshots, contact channel | Consistent identity across profiles |

G2 offers a free profile. Do not assume a free profile includes a followed outbound link or guaranteed search visibility. Evaluate these profiles primarily for buyer discovery and credibility, not purchased ranking signals.

## Days 15–30: earn links to useful resources

Prioritize assets already on the site:

| Asset | Relevant audience | Why an editor might cite it |
|---|---|---|
| `/tools/review-link-generator` | Local marketing agencies and small-business help centers | Solves the practical problem of finding a direct review link |
| `/resources/negative-review-templates` | Restaurant, dental, salon, and service-business educators | Gives owners adaptable response examples |
| `/resources/local-seo-checklist` | Chambers, business associations, and agency resource libraries | Provides a checklist their members can use |
| `/blog/restaurant-owners-guide-to-google-reviews` | Restaurant consultants and hospitality newsletters | Industry-specific instruction |

Select **15 relevant prospects**, each with a specific existing resource page and a clear reader benefit. Verify the page, editorial contact, and relevance before drafting. Prepare at most five personalized pitches per week. These are workload targets, not promised link placements.

Draft outreach, for owner review before sending:

> Subject: A practical review resource for [specific audience]
>
> Hi [name], your [specific article] explains [specific point]. We maintain a [tool/checklist] that helps owners [concrete task]. It may be useful alongside your section on [topic]: [direct URL]. You're welcome to try it and include it if it helps your readers. Happy to clarify how it works.

Avoid bulk templates, artificial urgency, and requests for exact-match anchor text. One relevant follow-up is enough; respect opt-outs.

## Days 31–60: create evidence worth referencing

1. Publish one customer-approved case study with a real baseline, measurement period, method, and results. Confirm consent to name the business and use its screenshots. Do not treat demo data or illustrative brand logos as customer evidence.
2. Ask an existing customer or partner whether the case study is useful to its audience. Offer a factual collaboration story; do not require a backlink as a condition of service.
3. Prepare one small original study using permissioned, aggregated data. State sample size, dates, selection criteria, and limitations. Never expose private reviews, customer identities, or tenant data.
4. Add contextual internal links from these assets to the matching feature and industry pages. Use normal descriptive wording.

## Days 61–90: build on what produces qualified visits

Review referral traffic, engaged visits, demo bookings, and trial conversions by referring domain. Expand the two topics that attracted useful readers. Update resources with clearer examples and pitch the additions only where they fit an editor's existing coverage.

Keep a simple tracker: prospect domain, exact source page, audience fit, asset URL, draft status, sent date, response, live link URL, link attributes, referral visits, and qualified conversions. Record Search Console's linking-domain trend monthly; its report can lag.

## Quality rules

Use editorial references, legitimate product listings, and real partnerships. Avoid purchased ranking links, private blog networks, automated directory blasts, fake reviews, and forced reciprocal exchanges. Any sponsorship must be disclosed and its links appropriately qualified. Google's [link-spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam) explain why these shortcuts are unsuitable.

The first 90 days aim to establish accurate profiles, produce two evidence-backed assets, and build a small pipeline of relevant editorial relationships. Neither a placement count nor ranking improvement is guaranteed.
