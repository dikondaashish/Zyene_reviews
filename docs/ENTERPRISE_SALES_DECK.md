## 2. The problem

- Local and regional brands lose revenue when Google ratings slip or negative reviews go unanswered.
- Enterprise tools (Birdeye, Podium, Reputation.com) price out SMBs and mid-market operators ($300–$500+/mo per location).
- Marketing and ops teams lack a single dashboard for requests, replies, and competitor benchmarks.

---

## 3. Solution

Zyene Reviews centralizes:

1. **Review monitoring** — Google, Facebook, Yelp in one inbox  
2. **Automated review requests** — SMS, email, and link flows after service  
3. **AI-assisted replies** — 1,500+ smart replies/month on Starter; unlimited on Enterprise  
4. **Negative feedback shield** — Route low ratings to private resolution first (ethical, no gating)  
5. **Competitor tracking** — Benchmark rating and review velocity  
6. **Embeddable widgets** — Carousels and badges for your websites (white-label on Enterprise)
